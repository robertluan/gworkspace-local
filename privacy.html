Privacy policy

gworkspace-local is a personal tool with a single user, Robert Luan (the developer).

  • It accesses Google Sheets, Google Docs and Gmail (send only) for the developer's own Google account.
  • Data stays on the developer's own computer and in his own Google account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
